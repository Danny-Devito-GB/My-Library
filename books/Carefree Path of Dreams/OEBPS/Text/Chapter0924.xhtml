Chapter 924: Subdue

Just how frightening would a complete Demon God be in this world?

One example was the Impermanence Great Dao that even Demon Gods could not do anything about.

Assisted by the power of his stats window, the current Fang Yuan, even if he had not recovered to his peak form, still had at least seventy to eighty percent of his original combat strength.

This was equivalent to an almost complete Demon God, which greatly surpassed the Lord of the Void's expectations.

The Four Mysterious Seals Array was not a burden to him at all. In fact, even the seals laid down by the Lord of the Void did not restrict Fang Yuan at all.

At this moment, he caught the Lord of the Void off guard by soaring into the sky and drowning the Lord of the Void with the destructive dragon flames from his mouth.

The blazing flames contained the power of the Dao of Creation and Omnidirectional Divine Demonic Armament Dao. Even the Impermanence Great Dao started trembling immensely, as though it had sensed great danger.

Kaboom!

The cone-shaped flames spread across the sky. Under the blazing temperatures, Terminator Number One was completely unable to fight back as it turned into ashes almost immediately, exposing the Lord of the Void's translucent body that was formed of many warped souls.

"How dare you…" the Lord of the Void roared. But the very next moment, he saw Snow standing on top of the red dragon with a smile and then leaping toward him.

A collapsing mountain peak and a radiance piercing through the sky. A young man locked in a confrontation with a figure formed of many twisted faces!

Everything was the same as the picture in the fax of the future!

"I want you dead!"

The Lord of the Void flew backward, and a formless force was already holding on to a slightly deformed revolver aimed at Fang Yuan.

Gun of Extinction!

The unique peculiarity of this Derivative was also one of the top few among all the Derivatives. And it had actually survived the flames!

After all, the flames that Fang Yuan had spat out were to kill and injure, not really to refine the energy of a Great Dao.

And this was the Lord of the Void's hope in making a comeback!

A shot to destroy your soul!

"I've already guessed that you would do that!"

While laughing out loud, Fang Yuan blinked. All of a sudden, a palm reached out toward the Lord of the Void.

Derivative! The Third Hand!

The ability to steal an object from the target without exception!

What does the present Lord of the Void still have on him? After Terminator Number One turned into ashes, all he has left is his void body and the Gun of Extinction!

This means that the items left to steal are either the Gun of Extinction or him himself!

The Lord of the Void in his current state had nothing else on him.

Under the two choose one premise, the randomness of the skill turned into a major probability!

Swoosh!

As a radiance flashed by, the Gun of Extinction appeared in Fang Yuan's hand.

He chuckled, aimed at Lord of the Void, and pulled the trigger without hesitation.

Bang!

A gunshot rang out!

The Gun of Extinction accurately shot the Lord of the Void.

From all corners of the world, it felt as though a desperate cry rang out. "No!!!"

In that instant, all his souls were erased. Just like that, the ruthless and myriad Lord of the Void who stopped at nothing disappeared from this world.

"The ability of the Gun of Extinction might have hurt his main body, but for the Lord of the Void in the Spiritual Realm who has all his ability as a Demon God, who knows how much damage he suffered…"

Fang Yuan blew the gun's muzzle as the red dragon let out a roar. Using his own strength, he started operating the array.

Four Mysterious Seals Array!

Even though Fang Yuan was still slightly lacking, he was still a Demon God who had recovered the majority of his strength! He could single-handedly take on the combined efforts of the two Demon Gods!

Chichi!

The strength of the black hands increased by yet another fold, and it started twining around the Impermanence Great Dao like the hairs on a head.

The blood-red flames burned, causing the two Demon God incessant grievances.

"Snow… Let us go!"

"We'll swear upon our True Spirits that we'll be grateful toward you, praise you, and let you order us around for ten thousand years!"

…

Arthur and Heinze cried out immediately after seeing the drastic change in situation.

"Swear upon your True Spirits that you will never divulge news about me and that you will give your all to do three things for me at no cost. Also, you will devote all the nomological power that you've absorbed here to me, and then I'll let you off!"

Fang Yuan rolled his eyes as he continued, "You guys should have seen it. The one plotting against you guys was the Lord of the Void. I have no malicious intent toward you two!"

"You… all right! I promise!"

The two Demon Gods were smart enough to know that there was not much to consider. Therefore, they immediately agreed to his terms.

By giving up all of this, it was equivalent to giving up on everything they had attained from this world. Additionally, they would even have three serious debts weighing them down.

But regardless, it was still better than perishing here.

Moreover, Demon Gods were ruthless and vicious. In a situation where Fang Yuan held an absolute advantage, they already knew what he would do to them if they rejected. It was without the slightest of doubt that Fang Yuan would continue what the Lord of the Void was going to do to them—strip them of their Great Daos, sacrifice them to the world, and subdue the Impermanence Great Dao!

"In the name of our True Spirits!"

The two Demon Gods swore as they spat out colorful radiances.

"Very good, let's go!"

Fang Yuan waved his hand, and a gap immediately emerged in the two light cocoons. Arthur and Heinze escaped desperately.

"Damn it… My nomological power and my accumulation of strength…"

At this very moment, the two Demon Gods exchanged glances with each other. They almost spat up blood. "Lord of the Void, we'll never forgive you!"

"I've decided to leave this world as soon as possible!" Arthur said in an unquestionable tone. "The array earlier extracted more than half of my strength and even threatened the natural source of my Great Dao!"

"This is really a massive loss…"

Heinze sighed and looked at the void.

In the void, Snow was riding his red dragon and standing opposite the Impermanence Great Dao.

"It seems like Snow is still interested in trying to subdue the Impermanence Great Dao… However, he isn't trying to use the Lord of the Void's exchange method. Instead, he's going to use brute force!"

For Demon Gods such as the Lord of the Void who only had twenty to thirty percent of their peak strength, forcing the Impermanence Great Dao into submission was never in their consideration. They had merely tried to lure it with something beneficial.

However, Fang Yuan was different!

After regaining a large portion of his original strength, he was indeed capable enough to try using brute force to subdue it!

"This is a battle between two Demon Gods. We're way too close to them, and it might be dangerous for us!"

Arthur and Heinze retreated, all prepared to leave this world.

However, their gazes subconsciously converged at the sky.

"I wonder how he did it. He clearly arrived not too long ago, yet he managed to recover a great portion of his strength. What kind of secret technique is it?" Heinze sighed.

"This is already beyond the secret technique category. I bet it's an ability of Netherheaven Demon Gods! This is probably why he wants us to keep it a secret!" Arthur shook his head. "This Demon God must have some huge secret on him!"

"Because of our vows, we won't be able to say anything to anyone… And that Lord of the Void…" Heinze shivered slightly. "I wonder if he received any news… After all, the Gun of Extinction is way too powerful. All of his souls and concealed hands in this world would have been erased on the spot. It's hard to tell if he managed to send out a message at the very last moment!"

"Who knows…" Arthur forced a bitter smile. "Either way… I do know something. The Lord of the Void is definitely going to be in trouble in the future!"

At the thought of how vicious and ruthless Fang Yuan was, Heinze could not help but shudder even though he was also a Demon God himself.

…

"Even though the Lord of the Void has a lot of tricks up his sleeves, this Four Mysterious Seals Array is the real deal and possesses the ability to restrain Great Daos!"

At this moment, Fang Yuan was all fired up and operated the array by himself in an attempt to restrain the Impermanence Great Dao.

"Impermanence! Impermanence!"

The Impermanence Great Dao was constantly fluctuating and trembling. However, this array was guarded strictly by the red dragon, and there was no way to break through it!

"Indeed… Even though the Impermanence Great Dao is comparable to a Demon God, it's not a true Demon God!"

A true Demon God would have their own intelligence, experience, and methods.

This Great Dao was still merely a Great Dao, just like a very primitive AI. It only knew how to react to the outside world passively. It was extremely sequential and inflexible without intelligence or state of mind.

Perhaps it could defeat a Demon God like the Lord of the Void, but Fang Yuan had the utmost confidence that he could take it down.

His only worry was still this world!

"You won't be able to escape!"

The red dragon roared, and two Great Daos appeared like long rivers engulfing and binding the Impermanence Great Dao.

One of the Great Daos was shining brightly with splendor, and that was the Creation Great Dao. The other contained the sounds of a powerful army, and that was the Omnidirectional Divine Demonic Armament Dao.

The two Great Dao surrounded the Impermanence Great Dao on the left and the right, bringing with them immense energy of attraction.

"Unbelievable!" The two spectating Demon Gods almost dropped their jaws when they saw what happened. "Forget about that Omnidirectional Divine Demonic Armament Dao, but that Creation Great Dao… Myriad Creation, why haven't we ever heard about it or its power? This isn't any weaker than any of those known Great Daos in the Mental Demon Realm!"

"Omnidirectional Demon God! Myriad Creation! Impermanence Great Dao, quickly surrender!"

Fang Yuan could not be bothered to deal with these two outsiders' shock. The two Great Dao extended outward. In an extremely aggressive and overbearing manner, they were about to take in the Impermanence Great Dao.

Kaboom!

At this moment, thunder boomed from the sky.

Streaks of lightning danced about in the sky in seven-colored mysterious radiances.

This was the power of annihilation of a high-level world, and it contained the will of the entire world!

"This world… You can't stop me, just like how you couldn't stop me from getting rid of the seal earlier!"

Fang Yuan laughed heartily.

However, he still made his move.

All of a sudden, a good amount of nomological power and the illusions of the two Great Daos that the Lord of the Void had extracted from the two Demon Gods gathered in the Four Mysterious Seals Array. Mixing with the power of many blood sacrifices, it condensed into the shadow of a Dao path that flew into the sky.

"This is…" Arthur's and Heinze's faces twitched. "A Pseudo Dao? No! A Pseudo Dao is nothing compared to this! The main ingredients are our previous losses… He's using them to fool the Heavenly Dao of this world? No way?"

The Lord of the Void's initial plan was to use the three real Great Daos as a sacrifice for the heavens.

In contrast, Fang Yuan was basically trying to fool the heavens! The Heavenly Dao was bound to be infuriated!

Of course, he would gain a little time before anything happened.

As the lightning streaks in the sky turned sluggish, the seven-colored Dao path rose unceasingly.

Making use of this opportunity, Fang Yuan extended his spiritual will with all his might. With immense pressure and oppressive energy, the Creation Great Dao and Omnidirectional Divine Demonic Armament Dao rolled toward the Impermanence Great Dao. It disappeared in the twinkling of an eye!
