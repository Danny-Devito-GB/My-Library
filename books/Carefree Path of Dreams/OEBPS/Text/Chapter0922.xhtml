Chapter 922: Four Mysterious

Poof!

A loud sound rang out.

Arthur stretched out his arms and put one into Terminator Number Two, while the other held the Memory Stick.

On another side, the Lord of the Void grabbed someone nearby and blocked Terminator Number One's shot. He opened his mouth, and a stream of black light fell onto Terminator Number One, quickly taking control of the Terminator.

Seeing this, Fang Yuan had Snow, his clone, candidly walk forward and take away the Fax Machine and the Bunshinsaba.

Heinze suddenly retrieved his focus and shouted, "Leave that door for me!"

"It seems that you were hit by the white flash. Are you okay?" The Lord of the Void smiled strangely.

"It's not a big deal. It just made me blank out for a while and lose one of my techniques!" Heinze had an awful look on his face.

All around, the Seven Color Scripture members, regardless of whether they were friend or foe, had all turned into idiots, their eyes pure like babies.

"Luckily, we're Demon Gods and can protect ourselves from that. Otherwise, we would have been the same as them!"

"This damn world…" Arthur said with some lingering fear eating at him. He could not imagine what would happen to him if he were not a Demon God.

If they were in the Spiritual Realm or the Mental Demon Realm, with the combined strength of four Demon Gods, it was enough to cause pandemonium. Be it Primary Color or Derivatives, they would decimate however many came.

However, they were in a high-level world that was on the same level as the Spiritual Realm and the Mental Demon Realm. This world innately suppressed foreign Demon Gods, and each of them could only display twenty to thirty percent of their strength.

On the other hand, the Impermanence Great Dao was equivalent to a complete Demon God, and the most powerful peculiarities of Derivatives were equivalent to twenty to thirty percent of a Demon God's full-powered strike.

Thus, these Demon Gods were naturally afraid of this world's Derivatives.

Nevertheless, things were different for Fang Yuan.

Having recovered more than half of his strength, even if the Memory Stick hit him, his brain only blanked out for a second, and he did not forget anything.

He even felt that he could directly fight against the Gun of Extinction.

Of course, only idiots would test such things.

"It seems that there were some communications earlier, but the connections were completely severed…"

Arthur played with the Memory Stick in his hand and glanced at Terminator Number One, who the Lord of the Void's had possessed. It was obvious that he was fearful of the Gun of Extinction.

"Of course, they needed to sever the connections. Otherwise, they would have been charmed by you!"

Fang Yuan shook his head and looked at the old man on the master seat. "How should we deal with him?"

The leader of Primary Color opened his eyes. "What… are… you?"

After observing them for a short while, he was sure that they did not have the intrinsic qualities of humans.

"Hehe…" Heinze walked forward, placed his right hand on the old man's head, and stroked it gently. "You don't need to know about our existence because it would be blasphemous to us!"

Ping!

As soon as he finished, he pulled out the old man's head as if he were picking a watermelon.

"Hmm… we're more or less done with the headquarters' defenses!" Fang Yuan clapped his hands. "What should we do now? Take away the Derivatives?"

"No!" The Lord of the Void stood up. "Do you guys still remember your promise? It's now time to help me do something!"

"You want to draw out the Impermanence Great Dao? Right here?" Arthur asked in disbelief.

"That's right. Those ignorant souls can be the offerings for my ceremony. What is more important is to draw it out," the Lord of the Void said. "From my recent observations, as well as my failure last time, I have summarized the characteristics of the Impermanence Great Dao… Derivatives require balance! If we destroy more than a hundred Derivatives at once, the Impermanence Great Dao will come out eventually!"

"Destroy more than a hundred Derivatives at once?" Heinze narrowed his eyes. "You have such a powerful secret technique?"

He had a secret technique that could rapidly digest Derivatives and refine their nomological rules. He had even tempted Fang Yuan with it once.

However, he did not know that Fang Yuan was powerful enough to destroy Derivatives directly using the power of a Great Dao.

"That's right. With my secret technique and Doroin's refining technique, we'll be able to extract the nomological rules of more than a hundred Derivatives and destroy them in one shot to attract the Impermanence Great Dao," the Lord of the Void said.

"But how do we subdue it afterward? It's as powerful as a Demon God after all," Arthur questioned.

The Lord of the Void had lost badly when he had faced the Impermanence Great Dao after meeting it by a stroke of luck.

In this world, they currently had less than thirty percent of their original strength. Even if they combined their powers, they were still too weak to contend with a Demon God.

"Therefore, we need an array!" Terminator Number One, who was possessed by the Lord of the Void, said passionately. His eyes were burning with fire. "I designed an array for it! There are four array centers. As long as you help me, we'll be strong enough to suppress the Great Dao!"

He looked at Fang Yuan, hinting that he needed his help.

"I see… okay!" Fang Yuan nodded. "We can give you an opportunity to subdue the Impermanence Great Dao, but before that, you have to give us the map of your array and your secret technique!"

"Yep! I agree!" Heinze echoed.

Arthur's eyes flashed without voicing any objections.

"No problem!" The Lord of the Void laughed loudly, clearly excited.

Fang Yuan looked down and touched the red lizard on his hand. His eyes flashed a bit.

"What is that on your hand, Snow?" Heinze gazed at the red lizard. "I noticed that strange thing quite some time ago!"

"Just a cute pet!" Fang Yuan smiled lightly.

"In Primary Color's overseas department, I remember there was a sealed derivative that was a sea dragon. It's said that it came from some lizard island. Your lizard should be a similar Derivative. At a critical moment, you can use it for defense!"

The Lord of the Void casually said, "How about we destroy it together with the rest?"

"If you are willing to destroy your Gun of Extinction, I hand over all my Derivatives!" Fang Yuan challenged with a teasing look. The Lord of the Void looked disappointed.

It was certainly important to extract and refine nomological rules for Demon Gods like them, but it was also perfectly fine to keep some suitable Derivatives to be part of their combat strength.

For example, Arthur would not hand over his Memory Stick.

Similarly, the Lord of the Void would not give up his trump card, the Gun of Extinction.

Meanwhile, Fang Yuan was quite interested in Derivatives that had clairvoyant nomological rules such as the Fax Machine and the Bunshinsaba.

To put it simply, ordinary Derivatives were not worth keeping, and they were usually refined directly. However, the genuinely powerful ones were best kept by the Demon Gods to put them at ease.

"The array that I'm going to use was completely developed by myself. It is called the Four Mysterious Seals Array. There's also the Hundred Psyche Refinement Array!"

The Lord of the Void straightforwardly told them about his arrays.

The four Demon Gods started setting up the arrays in the Primary Color headquarters.

Heinze had also taken out his refining secret technique without any hesitation.

However, this did not bring any benefit to Fang Yuan—Heinze had already promised to teach him this technique. Now, he had to share this technique with the other two.

"Although I did not have a plan in the beginning, I think it's best to start with a blood sacrifice after some thought!"

The four Demon Gods stood at four corners outside the mountain.

With a strange smile, the Lord of the Void ignited a blood-red flame.

Rumble!

The flames burned faster and faster, spreading all the way to the center of the mountain, burning the dead bodies of Primary Color's members and even those still alive…

The blood-red flames continued spreading across the area, engulfing the region.

Finally, it arrived at where the Derivatives were stored.

The prisons and seals were opened, freeing the Derivatives.

But soon after encountering the flames, they stagnated and started melting.

Lines of nomological power were extracted and flew into the sky before four black holes eventually engulfed them.

"The Impermanence Great Dao is really amazing. It can continuously actualize nomological objects!" Fang Yuan said poignantly while he was absorbing the nomological power together with other Demon Gods. "However, this precious stuff is limited… Just like how we can only attract the Impermanence Great Dao by destroying so many Derivatives at the same time… If we did this a few more times within a short period, would the Impermanence Great Dao weaken? In that case, it'd be much easier for us to capture it!"

"That won't work. Just like how origin power will never deplete, the power of a Great Dao can rapidly recover as long as it stays in its world, even if its nomological rules are split up!" Arthur disagreed. "Otherwise, the power of the Impermanence Great Dao would have already reached its nadir since it has actualized so many Derivatives!"

"I agree!" the Lord of the Void said. "If we want to subdue the Great Dao of Impermanence, the only way is to suppress it with sheer force!"

Kaboom!

The mountain with the Primary Color headquarters suddenly shook. Boulders tumbled down from above, as if the mountain was going to collapse.

Actually, this was not surprising. After all, this mountain had experienced a lot—the battle earlier, and the array with the blood flames set up by the four Demon Gods.

Along with the setting of the sun, nomological power started to flow out from the fractures in the mountain, appearing more magnificent than ever.

"Now! Four Mysterious Seals Array!"

The Lord of the Void roared, and a huge array appeared under his feet. The array formed a circle on the ground with the other three Demon Gods inside.

"Array core… and some attracting and sealing powers?"

Fang Yuan grew serious when he saw this, becoming secretly alert.

Even without the fax's reminder, he could tell that the Lord of the Void was up to no good!
