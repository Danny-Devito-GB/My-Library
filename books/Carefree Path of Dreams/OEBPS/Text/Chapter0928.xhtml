Chapter 928: Void

Even for a Demon God, the Lord of the Void was special.

He had billions of incarnations, and he did not have a main body at all.

In other words, every incarnation was equivalent to his main body.

When a large number of his souls accumulated, he would strength equivalent to a Demon God.

Naturally, he had weaknesses too.

For instance, when he met a strange object that was capable of defeating his incarnations in another world!

A large number of those incarnations were very weak. An attack like the one from the Gun of Extinction was more than enough to eliminate all of them at once.

One could say that the Lord of the Void's losses this time were even greater than having his cultivation sealed by the Celestial Thearch.

The huge Gu cultivation scene before Fang Yuan was all for his recovery and evolution.

As multiple souls merged again, they temporarily turned into his main body.

Unfortunately, Fang Yuan did not give him the time nor the opportunity. Instead, he launched an attack immediately.

"It's you again! Dream Demon!" Lord of the Void hollered. "You got in my way in the previous world, and this time, did you come to fight me, an inextinguishable existence that spans numerous worlds, one that is undying and endless?"

"Lord of the Void, you don't have to boast like this. After the fight in the previous world, I learned about your weaknesses. My Great Dao is perfect for fighting against you!"

Fang Yuan's expression did not change as the Impermanence Great Dao surfaced.

One of his Daos flowed out, carrying the strong aura of destruction. In an instant, it transformed into the shape of a sword.

"I can't believe you've absorbed the Impermanence Great Dao! Is that the Gun of Extinction?!"

The Lord of the Void's multiple incarnations let out their terrified screams simultaneously.

Peculiarities were an intrinsic quality, and weapons were nothing more than a medium. Even though Fang Yuan used the sword form that he was most adept in, with the Lord of the Void's senses, he recognized that this was none other than the 'Gun of Extinction'!

Fang Yuan held the Impermanence Destroyer Blade and asked calmly, "I have the Impermanence Great Dao. What's so strange about creating something with a similar peculiarity?"

Multiple souls accumulated at once. The Lord of the Void was clearly terrified of the Gun of Extinction. "Dream Demon… you can't do this. Did our alliance mean nothing to you? You are one of my allies, and we have the same enemy—Demon Supremacy Wish Fulfillment!"

The Lord of the Void's tone turned into a negotiating one.

"Demon Supremacy Wish Fulfillment? You've secretly joined hands with him long ago. If I ally with you, I'd be asking for my death!" Fang Yuan's voice was ice-cold. "On top of that, you were about to send me off as a blood sacrifice in the previous world. I won't forget that!"

"Dream Demon… I can't control all of my souls completely. I can't help that either…" The Lord of the Void hurriedly changed his tone. "Furthermore, you wiped out all my souls in that world. Isn't that enough for you?"

"Of course not!" Fang Yuan steeled his heart.

However, despite his words, the Lord of the Void did not utilize his strongest chip, Fang Yuan's secret. It seemed like his incarnations had perished too quickly, so the message had not passed on yet.

Even though the current Lord of the Void might not have known, Fang Yuan did not think that he would be able to fool the main body of the Lord of the Void.

Of course, all the consequences made his resolution to kill him even firmer.

"Dream Demon, you will regret offending me! As long as I'm still alive, you will spend the rest of your life wishing you were better off dead than alive!"

Multiple souls fused at once, turning into a huge illusory giant.

In the translucent giant, Fang Yuan could vaguely see a variety of twisted faces within.

This translucent giant was a body that was forcibly put together by the Lord of the Void, and it possessed half the strength of a Demon God.

However, the recovery process from before became utterly useless.

The Lord of the Void had no choice. In front of a genuine Demon God with the Impermanence Blade, every soul of his would be eliminated without question.

"Creation Sword Array, activate!"

Seeing this, Fang Yuan revealed a hint of a smile.

Nine terrifying sword lights charged to the sky. In an instant, it created a vast array that covered the entire region.

"Heaven, Earth, Mountain, Lake, Water, Fire, Wind, Thunder, Heart. Nine Swords, kill!"

The nine sword lights merged into one as they closed in on the Void Giant, piercing it ruthlessly.

"Ahh!"

The Lord of the Void's scream traveled far, and then there was only a deathly silence.

After the sword light pierced through its target, the Void Giant remained standing. His gaze revealed disbelief. "You've actually… advanced to Myriad Transformations? How is this possible?"

In the past, his opponent was nothing more than a mere Demon Master. Even the Demon God realm was something he attained by making a deal with him.

But at this moment, his opponent had skipped a thousand steps at once and joined the ranks of Myriad Transformations Demon Gods?

The Lord of the Void himself was at Void Amalgamation. All of a sudden, he felt like all the cultivation he had done in his life was utterly pointless.

"So what if you've advanced to Myriad Transformations? My Dao of the Void can turn reality into void. All of your attacks are useless against me!" the Void Giant hollered.

Every Great Dao had its unique feature, and anyone qualified to become a Demon God had a secret weapon up their sleeve.

However, Fang Yuan responded matter-of-factly, "Turning reality into void is something you can only do for a moment. If you turn into void continuously, you would turn into a complete void as well. To avoid that risk, that's the reason you created so many incarnations, right?"

"Anyway, the Dao of the Void isn't completely immune either. Otherwise, it would be undefeatable. After reaching a certain limit, an attack will be able to reach the void. When that time comes, won't you be injured?"

"Impermanence Blade!"

Swinging his right arm, the Impermanence Blade split open. Numerous black and white light particles dissolved into the Nine Swords.

At the Myriad Transformations realm, a single Dao could display thousands of Daos.

This meant that the unique peculiarities of the Impermanence Great Dao could integrate into the Creation Sword Array.

"Nine Swords, attack!"

In the next instant, the sword light turned into space, evolved into chaos, and swallowed the Void Giant.

"Dao of the Void, turn reality into void!"

The Void Giant roared, and its entire body turned into void.

After a moment, it turned into its physical form once again. The Void Giant laughed. "I told you, you can't defeat me!"

"The Dao of the Void is amazing. However, only your main body can use it. That means that you need at least half of your souls to assemble your main body before you can use the Dao of the Void…" Fang Yuan shook his head. "Otherwise, why weren't you able to have the last laugh in the previous world when you have this power? It's a pity…"

"Hmm?"

The Void Giant lowered his head. In an instant, it spotted a twisted soul. A black and white dagger was sticking out from its body.

For the Void Giant, an injury of this level was equivalent to being bitten by an ant.

But when the Void Giant saw this, it let out a loud scream.

On its body, multiple faces began to twist and implode.

That was the unique peculiarity of the Gun of Extinction: Extinction! It was able to kill all the souls at once, even across worlds!

While the injuries on his body stopped at once, the Lord of the Void's aura weakened at an extraordinary pace. The Impermanence Blade disappeared as well.

"The one who strikes first wins! I don't have to give you a heavy injury at all. As long as I create a small wound, it'll be enough!"

Fang Yuan held on to the Impermanence Blade, his smile as bright as the sun. "Of course, you have the Dao of the Void protecting you. Even the Extinction peculiarity is useless against you. But what about the souls in other worlds?

"In the previous world where the Gun of Extinction hit you, you received heavy injuries because it managed to extinguish the weaker souls in other worlds. It wasn't because it injured your main body. After last time, what about this time? If you take this level of damage a few more times, most of your incarnations will perish. What's left behind will be something completely unimaginable for you!"

The reason the Lord of the Void was difficult to kill was that he had billions of incarnations. He had laid down the foundations of his strength across different worlds.

However, the Gun of Extinction's unique peculiarity restrained that advantage completely.

After all, the remnants of his foundations would not be too powerful.

Even if they were in different worlds, after weakening the source of his power, he would eventually be annihilated!

By destroying all the foundations of his power, it not only destroyed the Lord of the Void's way out, but it would also cause him successive wounds.

Even though his losses were small, they were inflicted on his natural source. As they accumulated, it would eventually cumulate into a terrifying amount.

"Creation Sword Array, once more!"

With a wave of his hand, Fang Yuan gathered thousands of sword lights once again. Each one was strong enough to exterminate a Heaven Immortal and inflict massive damage to a Demon Master. And they landed on the Void Giant continuously.

"Your void state is getting shorter each time… Your injuries are accumulating. The extinguished souls will continuously aggravate your wounds, creating a vicious cycle!"

He had this in the bag. Now, he was taking his time to seal up the void, destroying the Lord of the Void's hopes for escape.

"No! You can't do this!"

As his incarnations continued to be eliminated, the Lord of the Void became terrified.

As the number of souls diminished, he started to feel the threat of elimination.

"For the current you, killing me will bring you no benefit! A Myriad Transformations Demon God won't be able to refine a Demon God's Great Dao. Furthermore, I have the Dao of the Void and can resurrect anytime. It'll do you no good. You'll just have another enemy that will never let you go! However, letting me live will be different. I can vow to help you deal with Demon Supremacy Wish Fulfillment!

The Lord of the Void added, "Did you know, Demon Supremacy Wish Fulfillment has a secret technique to refine a Demon God's Great Dao? Because of that, he has imprisoned the Emperors and Zenith Heavens. He has a great plan in mind. He may even have the power to break through the shackles of Netherheaven Demon God!

"I've made a deal with him once, and I managed to obtain a portion of the Four Mysterious Seals Array. It can extract a Demon God's Great Dao, but it can't refine it. Don't you want to know what happens next? Even though I don't know Demon Supremacy Wish Fulfillment very well, I do know everything there is to know about him. I can help you. I'll help you take everything from him!"

…

"That is very tempting. Unfortunately, I don't believe you at all!"

Fang Yuan's expression was ruthless.

This was an opportunity he could not let go of. If he had not injured the Lord of the Void heavily the last time, would he able to kill him now?

If he missed this chance, there would not be another one ever again. He might even have to deal with his vengeance in the future!
