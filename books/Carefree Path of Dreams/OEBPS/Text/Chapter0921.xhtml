Chapter 921: Peeking at Secrets

"Fireball!"

"Cone of Cold!"

In the underground headquarters, a group of special combat personnel—sorcerers trained by Primary Color—had arrived.

In addition, their incantations were extremely short. It was obvious that they had formed under short notice and had chosen to sacrifice confidentiality.

"Where is the Philosopher's Book?"

Heinze roared. Magic could not damage him in the slightest.

"I know… It's in the Primary Color headquarters!" A Primary Color sorcerer suddenly started laughing strangely. "You can find it if you head east! My men will update you along the way."

"Thanks, Lord of the Void!" Heinze replied gratefully.

He had laid low in this world for many years, even becoming a representative of the Sorcerer Congress. He had done all of this because he knew that the nomological rules in the Philosopher's Book were very useful to him.

He had been frustrated for quite a while when Primary Color got to it before him.

This time, he swore that he would let nothing go wrong.

"Tsk tsk… Rest assured. After you entered, I controlled the Terminator and reactivated Absolute Protection… Its protective powers can't be broken even by teleportation spells. You don't need to worry about people escaping using space manipulation techniques!"

The Lord of the Void cackled. "Right now… Arthur is enjoying himself as well. The Terminator with the soul resistance ability won't be a match for him!"

"Then I'll leave this place to you guys!" Heinze left Fang Yuan and charged toward a forked road.

"All right. Now, only you're left!" The sorcerer possessed by the Lord of the Void was wearing a strange expression. "Still remember… our agreement from earlier?"

"Yes. Do you need my help now?"

Fang Yuan shrugged nonchalantly.

"Although we had a small misunderstanding in the Spiritual Realm, you proved yourself with your actions. You aren't someone who acts on impulse. On top of that, you try your best once you've given your word…" The Lord of the Void licked his lips. "That's why I want to make another deal with you. As long as you help me, I can give you anything!"

"Don't make promises you can't keep!" Fang Yuan frowned. "Your goal is only to obtain the Impermanence Great Dao…"

"Great Daos are hard to find. Even though I've thought of a method, I'm only sixty to seventy percent sure that it'll work. This is actually a huge gamble for me too," Lord of the Void said. "Right now, I'm just adding some insurance. As for your compensation, I'll definitely give it to you once we're in the Spiritual Realm."

"Do my best to help you, huh? No problem!" Fang Yuan's eyes lit up. "Shall we make a contract?"

"How can we find the time now?" While they were talking, the Lord of the Void activated a technique and killed several reinforcements. "All right, what Derivatives do you want? I can help you with that!"

"Of course, I want the best ones!" Fang Yuan replied matter-of-factly.

"Turn left ahead and continue heading straight. That'll bring you to the core of this base. I advise you to be careful. The power that Primary Color possesses is definitely far beyond your imagination. There are two Terminators there, and one of them even has the Gun of Extinction," the Lord of the Void said, as if to remind him.

"I obviously won't be heading there alone!" Fang Yuan shrugged. "Aren't you and Arthur here? No matter what, we should defeat the master of this place first before we discuss the rest, right?"

"That's right!"

At this time, Arthur had arrived along with a large number of Seven Color Scripture troops.

He was surrounded by his original enemies, creating a strange sight.

Even if a Demon God's Great Dao was only slightly strong, it was sufficient to enslave any regular human.

The previous Arthur was nothing more than a small character. His current self truly showcased his terrifying side.

"The true Seduction Great Dao can even capture the most stubborn rock, and it would be willing to do anything for the Demon God. In fact, the sky, the ground, and eventually the entire world…"

Fang Yuan was not surprised by this. In this large army, he was like an ordinary person and strived to maintain a low-profile.

"Before this, I've captured at least half of the people in this place. Now that we're launching an attack, I'll use them at once. With Arthur's contributions, half of the base is already in our hands…" The Lord of the Void provoked him further. "As long as we can defeat the leader, this place will be ours. Everything inside, including the Derivatives and various intel, will become ours!"

"An enticing proposal!" Fang Yuan nodded. Suddenly, his right leg stopped.

After fighting through the grounds, they finally made their way to the core of Primary Color. The leader was in the large hall!

As long as Absolute Protection was running, nobody could teleport.

As a matter of course, the plan to use Teleportation or the Portal to transport Derivatives and personnel had yet to begin before it was strangled in the crib.

…

"Haha, the Philosopher's Book, I've finally obtained it!"

Heinze turned someone before him into ashes and took the book from his hands. He laughed aloud. "I have a strong feeling that as long as I consume the nomological rules in this, I will be able to reach the Myriad Transformations realm!"

He had been a Void Amalgamation Demon God for many years, pushing his Great Dao to perfection.

As long as he consumed these nomological rules, he could definitely evolve his Dao path into the Myriad Transformations realm!

Heinze put the Philosopher's Book away quickly. He listened carefully to the rumbling below, and his expression changed. "How could I miss out on this?"

A flock of black bats surrounded him as he transformed into black smoke and rushed to the frontlines.

Rumble!

Amid the violent rumbling, the final line of defense in the hall fell apart, unveiling everything before the Demon Gods.

The space was not large, and it had a round table with a projector on top.

There was only an old man taking a puff of smoke. He sat on the master seat while holding on to a fax paper with a serious expression.

The Lord of the Void spotted Terminator Number One and shouted, "The Gun of Extinction!"

Along with his line of sight, Fang Yuan noticed the strange gun in his hands.

"Attack!" Arthur laughed oddly while hiding behind his bewitched troops.

In terms of how he used his cannon fodder, Arthur was even looser than the Lord of the Void.

"The Future Fax Machine, the Bunshinsaba, and the Portal?"

Fang Yuan quickly scanned the area and spotted many things.

On the table, an ordinary pen stood straight up. As if it were being controlled by an invisible force, it was drawing circles.

Obviously, this leader had obtained some information from the future. He had felt greatly shocked and was attempting to use the Bunshinsaba to obtain further clarification.

"Attack!"

The Lord of the Void and Arthur controlled their respective 'subordinates'. These people used to be Primary Color's battle and research personnel, but under the two's orders, they charged forward.

"How pitiful…"

The old leader sighed and pressed a button on the table.

Unfortunately, nothing happened.

The next instant, the sound of gunshots and fighting ensued. Blood spewed, splattering across the entire hall.

"Tsk… you want to activate the hall's defense and use these machines to kill your former allies? Too bad, the base's main power source and the three back-up power supplies have landed in my hands!"

The Lord of the Void laughed loudly. "Of course, if you don't think it's too much trouble, I don't mind killing your subordinates for you… Hmm, since they're so loyal, I should reward them."

"Leader, go!"

The two Terminators stood next to the old man, watching their allies fall in front of them.

Of which, Terminator Number One observed the situation for a long time before he finally moved like lightning.

Bang!

He aimed the Gun of Extinction at the Lord of the Void.

A loud gunshot rang out.

Behind the Lord of the Void, an unlucky head exploded. Bits of red and white pieces splattered onto the ground.

The next moment, the unfortunate person disappeared from the face of the world, his body and blood entirely vaporized.

The people near him forgot his existence, seemingly like he was utterly wiped from this world.

"Impressive!"

Even Fang Yuan felt the aftershocks of fear.

At this moment, he could only recall the shadow of that unlucky man. Most of his memories about him were wiped clean.

This was even after he had recovered more than half of his power and was strong enough to use his Great Dao to restrain the nomological rules of this world.

The Lord of the Void and the other two vaguely felt that something was wrong, but it seemed like they did not realize that they had lost one of their allies. In fact, they were only a shoulder away from becoming that person.

But at this moment, the gun was loaded!

"The Gun of Extinction!"

Lord of the Void screamed and manipulated several puppets to go forward.

"It's mine!"

Arthur hid behind his forces while dashing forward.

Even Heinze could not hold himself back and joined the brawl.

The three Demon Gods knew clearly that the Gun of Extinction was a terrifying Derivative even among the others!

And it was strong enough to kill a Demon God in one shot!

All of them held evil intentions, so of course they would not yield the Gun of Extinction to anyone else!

"Number Two!"

Terminator Number One backed off. On the other hand, Terminator Number Two simultaneously stepped forward and pulled out a short, black baton.

"Memory Stick?"

Arthur was a little taken aback. He hurriedly shut his eyes.

Unfortunately, he was a moment too late.

A bright, white light flashed!

Nearly everyone who saw it was drawn into its effects.

"Damn it!"

Fang Yuan's reflexes were the fastest. As he had his Great Dao's protection, he was the first of the four Demon Gods to react to it.

However, he did not move. Instead, the red lizard on his back agilely crawled onto the round table and went toward the old leader.

The old man had his eyes shut tightly, not daring to make another move.

"This is…"

The red lizard lowered its head and clearly the contents of the fax clearly.

It provided a vivid image.

In the background, there was a collapsing mountain peak and a radiance piercing through the sky.

The main characters were a young man with three hands and a figure formed of many twisted faces.

Did the leader use the Bunshinsaba to get this…

Seeing this scene, a glint of light flashed past Fang Yuan's eyes. He spit out a small spark, burning the paper into ashes.
