Chapter 925: Splitting Treasures

In a split second, the Impermanence Great Dao that many Demon God yearned for landed in Fang Yuan's hands.

And at that moment!

Kaboom!

In the dark void, lightning bolts flashed wildly, emitting a terrifying force of annihilation. This was the fury of heaven's will.

"Of course… This is also normal. Just like when someone pays full price for an expensive feast only to be force-fed with a mouthful of pig's slop, he's bound to fight back with all his might!"

Fang Yuan's neck shrunk, as he had no intention to fight back. Without much hesitation, he chose to leave the world.

"Damn it! Let's quickly leave!"

Heinze's and Arthur's faces turned incredibly pale. "We can't visit this world in the future anymore! The Heavenly Dao's inspection and stifling of any alien species are bound to be incomparably harsh… What a turn off!"

Rumble!

But this was merely an exchange of thoughts.

At that moment, two purple lightning streaks descended upon their hosts' bodies.

Without the protection of a True Spirit and the strength of a Demon God, these two empty shells burned. Arthur and Heinze were naturally not going to bear the brunt of Fang Yuan's actions, so they had already escaped from this world.

…

In the Spiritual Realm.

In the boundless ocean.

A column of water suddenly shot up into the sky like a gigantic fountain.

Following the cry of a whale, a humongous whale suddenly leaped out of the water surface, causing incredible waves.

"Creation, Impermanence, and Omnidirectional Divine Demonic!"

Kaboom!

Three Great Daos emerged in the Spiritual Realm, causing instability in the weather. Dao Marks were constantly being carved into the void, and that was enough to cause low-level Earth Immortals and Heaven Immortals to scramble like mad ducks.

Fang Yuan appeared on the back of the whale as he watched the three Great Daos. His heart suddenly filled with a type of delight.

"Who would have thought… the Impermanence Great Dao could actually get on with my Creation Great Dao this well.

"Initially, I really did believe that the first Dao to be incorporated into my Creation Great Dao would be the Omnidirectional Divine Demonic Armament Dao. Who would have thought…"

His fundamental Dao path was a Dream Master's Creation, so naturally the Dao of Creation was going to be the center of everything.

"Creation Impermanence! What is it really?"

Fang Yuan pondered while watching how his Creation Great Dao accommodated the Impermanence Great Dao and how it was becoming increasingly faintly indiscernible and mysterious.

"Using the Dao of Creation to develop Impermanence… It feels like after adding the Impermanence Great Dao, my Creation Great Dao has become much more complete…"

He looked at the Omnidirectional Divine Demonic Armament Dao.

At this moment, this Great Dao no longer put up the same resistance as before, and it was also being incorporated into the Creation Great Dao.

Within this heaven and earth, there seemed to be only one Creation Great Dao capable of evolving scenes of Omnidirectional Divine Demonic Armament, Impermanence, and so on.

"Accommodating other Daos and using one Dao to evolve countless others… This is the Myriad Transformations Demon God realm!"

Fang Yuan knew that he had finally achieved the goal he had set out for during this dream-traversing.

A Myriad Transformations Demon God was an existence capable of being a tyrant wherever they went.

With this strength, he was finally confident enough to be part of the changes that were about to happen in the Spiritual Realm.

He took a quick glance at his stats windows:

"Name: Fang Yuan

Profession: Dream Master

Cultivation: Demon God (Myriad Transformations)

Dao Path: Creation Great Dao, Omnidirectional Divine Demonic Armament Dao, Impermanence Great Dao

Technique: Pangu Eagle Body [Strengthen Witch Bloodline (1st Tier)], Creation Sword Array [Ninth Sword (100%)]

Specialization: Botany [Level 6] (Maximum), Heavenly Eye Seer Spell, Body of Origin Power"

"I advanced by a small rank, but there are some troubles too…"

Fang Yuan's expression remained stern as he thought more about it.

The Gun of Extinction might have completely destroyed the Lord of the Void's incarnations in that world, but his main body in the Spiritual Realm might only be slightly affected.

In fact, he might even know about his information.

Being able to recover that quickly in a world like that was something only a Netherheaven Demon God could do.

As such, the other Demon Gods would naturally be extremely interested in this.

Even though Fang Yuan had already used contracts to ensure that Arthur and Heinze would not talk about it, he could not be so sure about the Lord of the Void.

"However… there is always a slight delay regarding the flow of information from an incarnation to the main body. He died too fast, so the information might not even flow to his main body in time… Regardless, the Lord of the Void will still probably know about what happened between us. He'll simply be uncertain about the details…"

Fang Yuan pupils contracted. "Perhaps… I should eliminate the Lord of the Void before meeting face to face with Demon Supremacy Wish Fulfillment!"

The Lord of the Void had clearly colluded with Demon Supremacy Wish Fulfillment.

Initially, Fang Yuan only had a smattering of knowledge about what had happened on the Mental Demon Battlefield, but after seeing the Four Mysterious Seals Array, Fang Yuan immediately understood Demon Supremacy Wish Fulfillment's intention.

This freak that was a fusion of Demon and Spirit was probably preparing to take down all the Emperors and Zenith Heavens in the Spiritual Realm before smelting all their Great Daos to eventually advance into Netherheaven!

A Myriad Transformations Demon God was capable of evolving other Daos using their own Dao path.

Meanwhile, a Netherheaven Demon God was an existence with an almost complete Dao path. Regardless of which world a Netherheaven Demon God descended upon, they would be capable of going against the oppression of the world to rapidly recover the majority of their strength.

Only a Demon God at this stage would be truly free.

This was because they could break away from the restrictions of the Mental Demon Realm to settle down in any world they liked.

Even though Fang Yuan had advanced to Myriad Transformations, he had only accommodated three Great Daos. It was still a long road ahead to raise his own Dao to a level comparable to a 'Heavenly Dao'.

"The majority of the Lord of the Void's arrangements are in the human realm. He is definitely not going to miss the fighting of dragons… The chess piece set up back then where Lin Shoucheng is can perhaps be a place to visit!

"Of course… the first thing to do is to start weaving in the Immortal Dao!"

…

Sky Horizon Sea.

Petals fell from the sky, golden lotuses sprouted on the ground, and numerous Dao Marks emitted myriad propitious vapors.

A simple and plain palace suddenly appeared before one's eyes. It was an awe-inspiring purple firmament palace.

"Teacher is starting his second lecture?"

The Penglai Island Master, Infinity Mystic Sect Master, and Luoshen Palace Master quickly arrived together. Once they entered the purple firmament palace, they greeted Supremacy Creation and addressed him as Teacher.

"All right!"

After eighty-one bell tolls, Fang Yuan waved his hands to separate heaven and earth and prevent anyone from further coming in.

"The Dao of Creation includes everything. What I am going to talk about today is the art of creating Impermanence!"

Fang Yuan spoke with a smile, saying each word articulately. Every word contained the mysterious energy of a Great Dao, causing the one thousand Golden Immortals, Heaven Immortals, and other low-level beings to almost seem as though they were intoxicated by his words.

"You can actually… do this?"

"Using the Creation Great Dao to evolve Impermanence?"

"Not only that, Creation includes everything in this world. This means that it can evolve…"

…

After one round of practicing, many Golden Immortals and Heaven Immortals praised again, "Teacher is truly remarkable. How I wish I can attend Teacher's lectures more often!"

"That's all!" Fang Yuan chuckled. "This is my very last lecture. It's entirely up to each and every one of you to absorb as much as you can before the arrival of the tribulation!"

"What?"

Their voice in unison was truly thunderous.

These Golden Immortals and Heaven Immortals who were starting to get used to Fang Yuan's lectures could not help themselves and fell to their knees. "Teacher… please don't leave us waiting here!"

"Did we do something wrong? Teacher, please point out our mistakes. We will surely change for the better!"

"Teacher… huhu…"

Some of the more shameless ones even started crying. After all, this was an extremely rare opportunity.

"Fools!" Fang Yuan displayed an awkward expression. "It is hard to go against heaven's will… I am already risking a lot by giving two lectures. Giving a third is out of the question!"

"Teacher!"

The Penglai Island Master and the other Golden Immortals also fell to their knees. Their current feelings were indescribable.

"However, we can be considered to be fated for all of you to have learned under me. I have some treasures that I have no use for any longer, so I will bestow them to you. This will be useful for you during the tribulation!"

Fang Yuan waved his hands, and a pile of black rocks appeared outside the firmament palace. Magnificent brilliances started circulating around the items, clearly the rays of treasure light.

"Mountain of treasures?"

The Luoshen Palace Master was bold and scrupulous, taking the lead to leave the scene. She arrived in front of the rocks and picked up a hossu. She waved it around casually and could feel the strong winds of the high heavens.

"Truly a great treasure!"

The Luoshen Palace Master could not contain the delight on her face as she grabbed another treasure, a five-colored robe.

"Out of my way!"

"Stop!"

How could the rest of the numerous Golden Immortals and Heaven Immortals stand a sight like this? They quickly charged out of the purple firmament palace and toward the pile of treasures.

If they were not afraid of offending the Dao Patriarch, some of them might have even executed all kinds of skills and moves here.

Eventually, the Golden Immortals gained the most advantage, as they had managed to snatch the majority of the treasures. However, some of the luckier Heaven Immortals had also managed to grab one or two good items.

In fact, some of the treasures had clearly chosen their own owners and flew toward their new owner.

"Thank you, Teacher, for bestowing us with treasures!"

The Penglai Island Master and the other two benefitted the most, so they were the ones to lead in appreciating Fang Yuan.

Fang Yuan stood at the entrance of the palace and addressed the crowd. "Don't worry about it. My treasures are fickle and mysterious, so be careful when you use them…"

"We will follow your instructions sincerely!"

The Luoshen Palace Master draped the five-colored robe over her shoulder and bowed to express her gratitude. All of a sudden, she cried out, "What's going on? This… is my voice?"

Her high-pitched voice had become extremely deep like a man's voice.

"Five Yang Robe. The person wearing it will turn from Yin to Yang (female to male), and it is irreversible!" Fang Yuan casually commented.

"What?" The Luoshen Palace frowned while looking heroic and exuberant.

She was still a Golden Immortal and had plenty of methods to transform her corporeal body.

Regardless, she was not going to take off the robe at a time like this.

"This robe has the protection of the Five Yang, and it can protect you from five Golden Immortal attacks. You better cherish it!"

Fang Yuan was chuckling in his heart but still appeared serious when he said that.

Following that, this bunch of disciples caused yet another ruckus testing out their new treasures.

"Very good… It seems like I can use the unique characteristics of the Impermanence Great Dao in this world too."

In reality, the giving out of treasures was Fang Yuan's experiment with Derivatives.

The truth proved that in the Spiritual Realm, where there were slightly fewer restrictions, the power of the Impermanence Great Dao was definitely usable.

"… The power of Derivatives is not sufficient to deal with Zenith Heavens. After all, it is merely nomological power, which will never be enough to deal with Demon Gods with a Great Dao protecting them… But they can absolutely kill anyone under Zenith Heaven immediately! Even a Golden Immortal wouldn't be able to withstand a blow from these!"

Seeing this, Fang Yuan could not help but nod. He was extremely pleased and satisfied with the performance of the Impermanence Great Dao.
